# Surface brief: public site (home, about, events, past events, gallery, contact, header, footer)

Mode: Persuade on home; Read on about; Operate on events, gallery, contact.
Audience: young European social-democratic activists and local party branches meeting EuropeTalks for the first time; returning members checking events.
Job: understand who EuropeTalks is (confirmed primary goal), then find an event or get in touch.
Constraints: keep logo, EU blue, quote-card yellow and watercolor textures (confirmed). No new claims or invented numbers. 13 languages incl. Greek and Ukrainian; long German/Hungarian strings. Light and dark themes. Shared ui primitives also serve admin.
Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a campaign poster for a real network. Europeans talking, shown with their own faces and words, on a field of logo blue. It refuses the darkened stock-photo hero with centred typewriter text and the three identical image cards.

OWN-WORLD: Logo blue #0B4392 drenches the hero, page heads, CTA band and footer. Navy ink #0A1A3A on a cool paper white. Marker yellow #F5CF4A is the primary action on blue fields and in dark mode, and the highlight colour for "EuropeTalks". On paper, primary buttons stay logo blue because a yellow fill on white has weak edges. The red, yellow, blue and green watercolor splashes bleed behind section headings. The logo's three white stripes are the structural rule. Sofia Sans Extra Condensed at weight 900, in caps, for display text; Sofia Sans for body. Event photos are full-bleed rounded tiles; the leadership portrait is framed as a print.

STORY: The visitor reads who we are in one sentence, sees real members speaking and real events, understands the mission, then explores events or writes to us.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Blue field merging with the header. Left 7 columns: a two-line condensed caps headline at clamp(3.5rem, 8vw, 7.5rem), the who-we-are sentence as the lede, then a yellow "Learn more" and a white-outline "Explore events". Right 5 columns: a stacked deck of member quote cards, slightly fanned, with the top card cycling.

FORM: Brand-led campaign poster (extension of an incomplete brand; no concept roll). Pinned by the user answer "Brand-led redesign: keep the logo, EU blue, yellow highlight and watercolor textures as fixed brand assets, but replace the generic look". Signature interaction: the quote-card deck shuffle.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
